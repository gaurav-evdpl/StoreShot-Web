About StoreShot Studio

Crafted for App Creators · Version 1.0

Beautiful apps deserve beautiful screenshots.

StoreShot Studio is a focused creative workspace that helps developers and designers turn real product screens into polished App Store and Google Play visuals.

Fluid & Focused Workflow

Import your screenshots, choose a device, add typography and backgrounds, organize multiple pages, and export store-ready images—all in one simple workflow.

Local-First & Privacy Conscious

StoreShot is built with a local-first approach, so your projects and screenshots stay on your device. No account is required to start creating.

StoreShot Studio is created and maintained by StoreShot Studio.
