Privacy & Trust

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026 · StoreShot Studio

StoreShot Studio is designed to keep your creative work private and on your device.

Your projects and screenshots

Projects, screenshots, text, layouts, preferences, and exported images are processed and stored locally on your device. StoreShot Studio does not upload your projects or screenshots to our servers.

The app does not require an account and currently does not include advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics.

Photos and notifications

StoreShot only receives the photos or files you choose through Apple's system picker. If you select Save to Photos, the app requests permission to add the exported images to your Photo Library.

If notifications are allowed, StoreShot may display a local confirmation after your images are saved. You can change Photos and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Website and support

If you contact us, we may receive your email address and the information included in your message. We use it only to respond to your request and provide support.

Our website host may process basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type, and request logs, to deliver and protect the website.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to provide website or email services, comply with the law, protect StoreShot and its users, or when you choose to export or share content.

Data deletion

You can delete projects inside StoreShot Studio. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data, subject to any backups managed through your device or Apple account.

You may ask us to delete information you sent through a support request by emailing support@storeshotstudio.app.

🔒 Privacy & Support Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact: support@storeshotstudio.app
